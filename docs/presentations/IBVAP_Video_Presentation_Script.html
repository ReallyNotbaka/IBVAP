Exact Codebase File & Line References:

src/ibvap/api/routes/cameras.py
Lines 42–135 (_camera_worker): PyAV decodes video frames into BGR arrays and encodes JPEG 70% quality.
Lines 87–127 (Auto-Reconnect): Catches stream drops, enters RECONNECTING, and retries with backoff instead of terminating the thread.
Lines 380–405 (camera_stream): Async generator polls _FRAME_VERSIONS non-blockingly (await asyncio.sleep(0.015)) without thread locks.
src/ibvap/core/detector.py
Lines 67–92 (DirectML Selection): Inspects ONNX Runtime providers and prioritizes DmlExecutionProvider to run on any NVIDIA/AMD/Intel GPU without CUDA dependencies.
Lines 108–133 (_preprocess): Aspect-ratio-preserving letterboxing to 640×640 with normalized offset coordinates.
Lines 162–189 (detect & NMS): Filters security classes with confidence threshold 0.48 and NMS IoU 0.45.
src/ibvap/core/tracker.py
Lines 32–35 (Footpoint Calculation): Calculates ((x1+x2)/2, y2) bottom-center ground contact point for accurate virtual fence intrusion detection.
Lines 108–132 (Cross-Class NMS Deduplication): If an object is detected as both Car and Motorcycle/Person, spatial overlap (IoU > 0.40) resolves to the higher confidence class, completely preventing duplicate ghost boxes.
Lines 134–137 (Age Culling): Only returns tracks with age <= 2, purging stale ghost boxes.
src/ibvap/core/pipeline.py & outbox.py
Lines 110–162 in pipeline.py: Emits lifecycle state transitions (zone_intrusion, target_entered, target_exited) rather than spamming alerts every 5 seconds.
Lines 29–50 in outbox.py: Atomic transactional outbox writes with deduplication key camera_id:zone_id:track_id:stream_epoch.
frontend/src/components/OverlayCanvas.tsx: Stable keys (key={`track-${b.trackId}`}) prevent jumpy box transitions; dual-stroke 2.2px halo provides all-terrain contrast.

Spoken Voiceover (Read verbatim):

"What makes IBVAP innovative is its radical independence from specialized hardware.

Under the hood, our pipeline ingests uncompressed RTSP and MJPEG streams through an asynchronous PyAV demuxer. Using DirectML acceleration, our models run on commodity silicon—from low-power edge boxes to off-the-shelf field laptops—without vendor lock-in.

To solve real-world false alarms, our custom tracker incorporates cross-class spatial deduplication, preventing dense scenes from generating overlapping ghost labels.

And by decoupling video streaming from telemetry polling via non-blocking version generators, our interface achieves fluid, zero-stall video performance even over constrained tactical networks."
